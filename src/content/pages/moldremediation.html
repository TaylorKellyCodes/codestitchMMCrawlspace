Health & Safety First

Expert Mold Removal for a Healthier Central NC Home

Mold in your crawl space affects your health and your home. In the humid North Carolina climate, mold spores grow on damp floor joists and insulation. Through the stack effect, those spores are pulled up into your living areas, which can cause respiratory issues and persistent musty odors.

At M&M Crawl Space Renovations, we provide full mold remediation that follows strict safety protocols so your home is clean and your air is healthy. Our team identifies the type of growth and stops the moisture source so the mold stays gone.

Our Remediation Process

  • Detailed Assessment: We measure the extent of the growth and use moisture meters to find the hot spots where mold is active.
  • HEPA Containment & Removal: Using HEPA filtration and vacuuming, we safely remove active mold and keep it from spreading to the rest of your home.
  • Antimicrobial Treatment: We apply professional-grade, eco-friendly antimicrobial treatment to kill remaining spores and help prevent regrowth on wood surfaces.
  • Root Cause Correction: We fix the source of the moisture. Whether you need a sump pump or a full crawl space encapsulation, we make the space inhospitable to mold.

Is It Time?

Signs You May Have Crawl Space Mold

Crawl space mold grows out of sight, so most homeowners first notice it upstairs. Watch for:

  • Musty or earthy odors: A persistent smell that is strongest near the floor or after rain is a common early sign.
  • Visible discoloration: Dark spots, streaks, or fuzzy growth on floor joists, subfloor, or insulation.
  • Worsening allergy or respiratory symptoms: Especially symptoms that improve when you leave the house.
  • High indoor humidity: Condensation on windows or ductwork can point to moisture feeding mold growth below.
  • Recent water intrusion: Any past flooding, plumbing leak, or standing water in the crawl space makes active mold more likely.

What Affects Cost & Timeline

Mold remediation pricing depends on the extent of the growth, how much of the crawl space is affected, and whether the moisture source also needs to be corrected as part of the job. We start with a free inspection so your written quote is based on what we find.

  • Extent of growth: Isolated mold on a few joists is a smaller job than widespread growth across the subfloor.
  • Moisture source correction: Fixing the root cause (standing water, missing vapor barrier, poor humidity control) adds scope and keeps the mold from returning. Encapsulation is the most common permanent fix.
  • Accessibility: Tight or obstructed crawl spaces take longer to treat.

Most remediation jobs are completed in one to two days. We walk you through what we find and what the fix involves before any work begins.
