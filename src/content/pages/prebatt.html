Energy Efficiency & Home Safety

Precision Insulation and Fire-Stopping for a Safer, Warmer Home

Many homeowners don't realize that their crawl space is the biggest "thermal leak" in their house. Without proper insulation and draft stopping, the winter air in the Triangle flows freely through your floor joists, making your HVAC system work twice as hard. Worse yet, many older homes in Raleigh and Durham lack the critical fire-stops necessary to slow the spread of flames between floors in the event of an emergency.

At M&M Crawl Space Renovations, our "Trustworthy Work" means we never cut corners on what you can't see. We provide precision-fit prebatt insulation to eliminate cold spots and professionally installed fire and draft stops to seal your home's thermal envelope. Whether you’re trying to lower your energy bills or ensure your home meets modern NC safety codes, our team delivers the expert installation you need. Insulation performs best over a dry floor system, so we often pair it with crawl space encapsulation or a sump pump and dehumidifier.

Our Compliance-Focused Process

  • Thermal Gap Audit: We identify exactly where drafts are entering your home and where insulation is missing or failing.
  • Precision Prebatt Installation: We install high-quality batt insulation with exact fits, ensuring no gaps are left to let heat escape.
  • Fire & Draft Stopping: We use code-approved materials to seal the "chimney effect" in your wall cavities, preventing air (and potentially fire) from moving between levels.
  • Final Quality Check: We verify that all work meets or exceeds local Raleigh and Durham building codes for your peace of mind.

Is It Time?

Signs You Need Prebatt Insulation or Fire/Draft Stops

Insulation and fire-stopping problems are easy to overlook because they're hidden inside your crawl space and wall cavities. Common signs include:

  • Cold or drafty floors: Floors that feel noticeably cold in winter often mean the joist bays below are missing or have failing insulation.
  • Rising energy bills: A crawl space that's leaking conditioned air makes your HVAC system work harder year-round.
  • Sagging, damaged, or missing batt insulation: Insulation that has fallen, gotten wet, or was never installed correctly leaves gaps in your home's thermal envelope.
  • An older home with no visible fire-stopping: If your home predates modern building codes, it may be missing the fire and draft stops required to slow fire spread between floors.
  • A home inspection or code review flagged missing insulation or fire-stopping: This is one of the most common ways homeowners first learn about the issue.

What Affects Cost & Timeline

Every home's crawl space and framing layout is different, so we always start with a free inspection rather than a phone estimate. Pricing depends on:

  • Square footage and number of joist bays: More area means more material and installation time.
  • Amount of fire-stopping required: Homes with more wall cavities needing sealing take longer to complete.
  • Condition of existing insulation: Removing old, wet, or damaged insulation adds to the scope before new material can go in.

Most projects are completed in a single day for an average-sized crawl space. We'll give you a clear, written quote after the inspection—no surprises once work begins.
